# Buffalo Cleaner

### See your storage. Clean with intent.

A native macOS cleaner built around a simple rule: inspect what will change before cleaning. Scan storage, group candidates, and review individual items before deciding what to remove.

## What you can do

- **See where space goes:** Smart Scan summarizes storage by data group.
- **Review developer leftovers:** find build caches, SDKs, and other developer junk alongside system and user caches.
- **Choose deliberately:** inspect candidate items and their categories before cleaning.
- **Review the result:** Clean History records what was cleaned.

The product is designed to move selected items to the Trash and record undo information before a clean. Items with low confidence are meant to remain visible without being preselected, and a confirmation sheet shows the final selection.
